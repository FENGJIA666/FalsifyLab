The entry's demonstrated capability should be attributed to this workflow, not to pre-existing mathematics or dependencies.

## Established background and reused tools

The scalar decay model, explicit Euler update and its non-expansion condition are established mathematics. Rational arithmetic uses Python's standard library. Browsers, Python, Node-based test execution, PDF rendering and video encoding tools are pre-existing infrastructure. The application interprets only its documented controlled-English grammar and runs without external service credentials.

## What the prototype demonstrates

A learner or scientific-computing developer can inspect a claim before running it, identify exact violations, distinguish a partial passing grid from full-domain analysis, narrow assumptions without erasing the original failure, and replay the resulting report. This is an implemented combination of review and provenance features around one known mathematical model. It is not evidence of first-of-its-kind research, a new theorem, general scientific language understanding or a measured productivity improvement.

## Limitations relevant to judging

The model family is deliberately narrow. Rational-grid search misses isolated irrational witnesses. Analytical coverage is specific to the stated scalar Euler property. Results do not establish physical-model validity. Impact and novelty must be assessed by judges; local correctness and format compliance do not establish competitive superiority. Submission status is tracked separately.
